Bring the full squad (see *Team comps* above — Second Master's Board allows the cap shown in the header). This is the leveling run: everyone gains rank XP, so follow the *cap − 3* rule from the Crucible overview if you're leveling the whole team.

## Safe route

**Right**, **left**, **right**, then **straight** at the final fork. The most consistent path with the fewest elites, and a campsite right before the boss. Paralysis resistance for you or your beasts helps on Lauda.

## Coverage roster

Golem, Worm, Ghost, Behemoth, Karlabos, Raptor, Opo-opo, Drake, Mantis, Colibri, Cu Sith, Gigantoad, Ice Golem, Adamantoise, Geshunpest. Looks daunting; once the mechanics are known it's fairly straightforward. IL 135 sync — you will want upgraded job gear.
