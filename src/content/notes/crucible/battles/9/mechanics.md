**Wyvern Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Coeurl](../../../bestiary/coeurl/), [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/), [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/) — Lightning damage and bulk.

## Abilities

- **Storm's Grip** — wind sprites (NW and SE) that periodically fire lines across the whole north and south — they persist all fight
- **Typhoon** — knockback plus tornadoes crossing the arena
- **Liquid Hell** — fire puddles that Burn — they stay down
- **Blazing Trail** — jumps to the east or west wall and fires a wide line
- **Storm Trail** — three cones marked by green sigils around the boss

## How to handle it

Sprites first, so everything after is dodged on top of their lines. For Blazing Trail run behind the boss, threading the Liquid Hell puddles — the sprites will telegraph while you're moving but you have time, so don't stop halfway.

The second Typhoon's tornadoes are noticeably bigger; give them more room.
