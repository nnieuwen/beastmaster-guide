**Corpse Flower Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/), [Wespe](../../../bestiary/wespe/) — often just a loot node; if it's a fight, burn it.

## Abilities

- **Budding Thorns** — three saplings with expanding thorns — slow you, but block draw-ins while you stand in them
- **Floral Trap** — 2 s bind then draw-in; if it lands she eats you and gains a damage buff
- **Rotten Stench** — stack marker (fine to take alone)
- **Acid Rain** — AoEs that follow you

### Queen Hawk Piece

- **Final Sting** — goes off if it isn't killed in time

## How to handle it

Stand in thorns for Floral Trap and don't be in front afterward. Trick: the trap will eat the **Queen Hawk** instead and kill it outright with no buff — position so it gets pulled and you don't.
