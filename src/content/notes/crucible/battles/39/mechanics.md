**Chimera Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — damage plus a cleanse.

## Abilities

- **The Dragon's Roar** — donut + a breath
- **The Ram's Roar** — circle + a breath
- **Rushing Roar** — tether; breath after the dash — stretch it purple at the edge
- **The Ram's Keeper** — marker: expanding ice puddle that freezes — drop at a wall and leave
- **Cacophony** — lightning orb that follows and explodes
- **Toxic Tantrum** — raidwide + cleansable poison

## How to handle it

Breaths by head: **lion = fire in front, dragon = lightning to the left, ram = ice to the right.** Read which head glows with each roar and dodge the donut/circle *and* the breath together.
