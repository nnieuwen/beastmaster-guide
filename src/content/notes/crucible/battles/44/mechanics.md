**King Ahriman Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — tankiness, AoE and a cleanse; Doom resistance, if you found it, lets you ignore most of this — and it drops very strong loot.

## Abilities

- **Roulette** — pulls and binds you to an hourglass with a rotating cursor; the hourglass cleaves along the cursor when it dies — kill it while the cursor isn't on you
- **Stare** — no cast bar; line tankbuster
- **Double Vision** — random debuff: Sad → stand in red, Angry → stand in white (World of Darkness rules; the debuff shows 1–2 s before the cast ends)
- **Curtains for Rank 5** — raidwide that kills any pet whose rank is a multiple of 5 — release it first
- **Flare** — proximity that also hits the pet — wall, away from the pet

### Adds

- **Dirty Eye** — gaze that Dooms (uncleansable) — look away
- **Hapalit** — cleansable Burn

## How to handle it

Let Gigantoad tank for a while. Meteor the summons.
