**Siren piece** with shambling and crawling pieces — the elite before the boss.

- Her **auto-attacks cleave** and the cleave hits your pet: keep her facing away from it.
- She jumps around — get to her **sides** after the first jump; then a **giant line AoE** through the middle.
- **Summon:** three normal adds run at you plus **one tethered zombie**. If it catches you: Damage Down + Bind, which forfeits Unbeastable. Avoid it actively, then burst everything with Cu Sith.
- **Dead Man's Dirge:** if she **huddles**, point-blank AoE — get out. If she **hovers** in place, it's a donut — get in. Watch the animation, not the name.

Source: [Mrhappy1227 — Third Board of the Unbroken LEGENDARY Rank Clear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt519BDzQqo), via transcript.
