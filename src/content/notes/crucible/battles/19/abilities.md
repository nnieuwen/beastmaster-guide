**Siren Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/) — Meteor for the zombies and Piercing damage on the boss.

## Abilities

- **Song of Torment** — tankbuster
- **Unmooring Melody** — untelegraphed and fast: jumps to a wall and channels a cone that stacks a debuff — Confusion at 16
- **Feral Lunge** — jumps to a wall, then a line
- **Summon** — three zombies plus one tethered to you that binds on contact
- **Dead Man's Dirge** — wings spread = donut, wings together = circle
- **Distant Tune** — three sets of exploding orbs
- **Inviting Verse** — forced march, paired with a Melody — resolves when she stops singing

## How to handle it

Keep a Shield Charge ready. When Unmooring Melody starts, **sprint to her side immediately** — it's a fast cast. Meteor the first Summon, read her wings for Dirge, and line up Inviting Verse so the march takes you back toward the centre.
