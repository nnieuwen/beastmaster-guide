**Gigantis Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/) — tankiness, AoE and an interrupt.

## Abilities

- **Gigantic Rage** — crouch with club in front = circle; club overhead in both hands = rear half cleave; one-handed swing = front half cleave. All knock back
- **Smashing Stamp** — marker: frontal line with a small red box near the boss that kills a slime standing in it and absorbs its element

### Adds

- **Slime** — casts Rupture — self-destructs unless interrupted; crushed slimes split into tiny ones (Shield Charge / Meteor)
- **Cyclops** — Camaraderie heals the boss 5% unless interrupted; frontal lines

## How to handle it

Borrow Soul Crush, swap to Gigantoad. Until you know the animations, stand off to the side and move on the telegraph. Bait Smashing Stamp onto a slime of the **opposite** element to the club (same element makes it grow); after the crush a lightning club leaves a tower under you (stay in), a fire club leaves an AoE (move). With two slimes: crush one, interrupt the other, crush it on the next Stamp.
