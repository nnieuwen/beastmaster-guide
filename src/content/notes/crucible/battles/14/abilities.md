**Cavalier Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Raptor](../../../bestiary/raptor/), [Squirrel](../../../bestiary/squirrel/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/) — Fire damage and AoE for the Bishops.

## Abilities

- **Steelripper** — frontal cone
- **Doubling** — absorbs up to five orbs to make clones
- **Menace** — large circle from each clone
- **Crushing Blade** — knockback + heavy damage — pet cover does not immune it
- **Valfodr** — arena-length line from each clone on a wall

## How to handle it

Opens with two Bone Bishops; kill them while dodging Steelripper. Two clones → Menace → find a safe corner; then Crushing Blade, so don't stand where the knockback puts you out. Second Bishop pair dies to Meteor; second clone set stands on a wall for Valfodr.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
