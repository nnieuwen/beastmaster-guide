**Cavalier piece & bone bishop** — the simplest fight on the board.

- Adds spawn; kill them **as soon as they appear** (Tempered Release + Parting Blow on one beast, then the next).
- Back into a corner so everything groups up, then finish with a combo. A gap-closer keeps them off your beast.

Source: [Mrhappy1227 — Third Board of the Unbroken LEGENDARY Rank Clear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt519BDzQqo), via transcript.
