**Strix Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/), [Wespe](../../../bestiary/wespe/) — raw DPS to skip as much as possible.

## Abilities

- **Plummet** — three rows of three AoEs — stand out of the first row and move in after
- **On the Properties of Quakes** — stand in the levitation circle
- **Magical Mallet Theory** — step in the slime puddle to become a slime (stunned, takes 1 damage)
- **On the Properties of Floods** — damage + knockback unless you are a kappa
- **Ultimate Focus** — dispellable magic damage-up
- **On the Properties of Darkness** — raidwide
- **Windfeather Whisper** — feather add with an unavoidable knockback — Shield Charge back in
- **Check Out** — books fire paired circles in the order their mouths open

## How to handle it

Each "Properties" cast has one answer (levitation circle, kappa form, slime puddle). Dispel Ultimate Focus if convenient — Darkness doesn't hurt much either way. Rotate away from the open-mouthed books.
