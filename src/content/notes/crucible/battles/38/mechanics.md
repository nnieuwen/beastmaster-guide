**Medusa Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/) — damage plus an interrupt.

## Abilities

- **Summon** — two lamias first, then one lamia + four cyclops
- **Petrifying Regard** — tethered / rotating petrify cone: buffs lamias it hits, petrifies cyclops, and **kills you outright**
- **Impassion** — self Vulnerability Down + damage-up, not dispellable
- **Rippling / Constricting Evisceration** — two sets of lines that fire in spawn order; Rippling = circle then donut, Constricting = donut then circle

### Adds

- **Lamia** — tries to raise dead lamias — interrupt with Soul Crush
- **Cyclops** — arena-wide circles on spawn; dies to any hit while petrified, then frontal lines

## How to handle it

Never bait the gaze onto a lamia. Do bait it onto cyclops — petrified ones die in one hit.
