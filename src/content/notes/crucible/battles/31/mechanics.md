**Borgny Piece** — boss.

**Suggested trio:** [Coblyn](../../../bestiary/coblyn/), [Diremite](../../../bestiary/diremite/), [Drake](../../../bestiary/drake/) — poison mitigation — with poison resistance from a shop or chest (e.g. Angel Robe) swap Coblyn and Drake for DPS.

## Abilities

- **Toxic Breath** — backsteps, then a lingering poison cone — get to the wall behind and stand to the side
- **Toxic Vomit** — four baited puddles — drop them on a wall; Vulcanize / Smoldering Scales or pet-tank if unresisted
- **Fuming Vomit** — AoEs that spread into eight moving clouds (slight knockback)
- **Wriggling Phlegm** — baited AoE that spawns a slime; it walks to the nearest puddle and explodes (damage + Damage Down)
- **Cauterize** — flies to a side, line divebomb with clouds, then a knockback from the middle
- **Salivous Snap** — tankbuster

## How to handle it

Poison resistance trivialises this fight — take it whenever the board offers it. Root the Wriggling Phlegm slime with Diremite's Silkscreen and burn it before it reaches a pool.
