**Piscodemon Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/), [Zu](../../../bestiary/zu/), [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/) — damage, Slashing via Zu, and tankiness.

## Abilities

- **Void Blizzard III** — exaflare-style circles marching south from the north wall in lanes
- **Clear Mind** — self damage-up (dispellable)
- **Arcane Blast** — unavoidable AoE
- **Void Flare Star** — proximity fireball — get away
- **Void Thunder III** — cross under you
- **Void Aero III** — donut under the boss

## How to handle it

Read the north wall for which lanes have circles, stand in an empty lane, then sidestep into a lane once its circle has passed you. Dispel Clear Mind (Bloodcurdling Caw or similar) before Arcane Blast. Like the Arch Demon it layers mechanics the longer it lives — kill it fast.
