**Piscodemon piece.**

- **Void Blizzard** — pulsing exaflares; dodge into the first one after it goes off.
- **Clear Mind** — buffs the boss, and the follow-up **Arcane Blast** is heavy raid-wide damage. Cleanse the buff with a beast skill that removes a beneficial status (Vulture's Bloodcurdling Caw) to blunt it.
- **Void Flare Star** — proximity AoE. Run away.
- **Void Thunder III** — large, slow plus-shaped floor AoE.

Source: [First Board of the Unbroken Crucible EASY Guide (Legendary Rank & Third Degree)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0_G1NCwhJ0), via transcript.
