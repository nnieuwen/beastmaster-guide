**Taurus Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/), [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/), [Chimera](../../../bestiary/chimera/) — Piercing damage and lots of bulk for tanking.

## Abilities

- **Mortal Ray** — Doom, 25 s
- **Ruinous Ring** — donut
- **Ruinous Locus** — circle
- **Ray of Ignorance** — an orb that pulses ever-larger circles — drop it in a corner
- **Aetherial Fissure** — sigils on the east and west walls fire lines across the arena; glowing ones fire first
- **Ruinous Contraction** — donut then circle

### Ahriman Piece (×4)

- **Gaze** — look away; applies Doom, then they become targetable

## How to handle it

Four floor squares light up as you stand on them and cleanse **Doom** when fully lit — that's the whole fight. Cleanse the opening Mortal Ray, dodge Ring → Locus, park Ray of Ignorance in a corner, cleanse the second Doom, then read the wall sigils for Aetherial Fissure.

Look away from the Ahrimans, then kill them. Ruinous Contraction is just Ring + Locus back to back.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
