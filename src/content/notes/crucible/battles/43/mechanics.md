**Behemoth Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Golem](../../../bestiary/golem/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Ghost](../../../bestiary/ghost/) — tankiness and a cleanse.

## Abilities

- **Meteor Impact** — drops five rocks on you; a rock explodes if too close to another — keep at least one for later
- **Lightning Barrage** — two rings to a tower — stand by a tower to transfer, step away when its circle appears
- **Charybdis** — arm raised = circle, on the ground = donut
- **Ecliptic Meteor** — you *and* your pet must each be behind a rock
- **Meteor** — multi-hit stack marker (no rocks)

### Adds

- **Lightning Sprite** — boss immune until all are dead; cross attacks and lightning puddles (the puddles charge towers, the crosses don't)
- **Giant** — Grand Sword — frontal cone that destroys rocks it hits

## How to handle it

Energy Towers gain rings from puddles and Barrage; interact to release, and at three rings they detonate and stun. Otherwise a standard Behemoth — it dies quickly.
