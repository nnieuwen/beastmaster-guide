**Lakhamu piece & golem piece.**

- Take the **knockback into a safe section** — line up first, dodge everything, then AoE-stun with Buffalo into a Vampiric Fang for the heal.
- Once the **earth shakers** are targeted, Parting Blow so no pet is out to take them.
- Loot: a Crucible item and a Beast Potion showed up here; a blind or petrification potion usually drops off this "second boss".

Source: [Mrhappy1227 — Third Board of the Unbroken LEGENDARY Rank Clear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt519BDzQqo), via transcript.
