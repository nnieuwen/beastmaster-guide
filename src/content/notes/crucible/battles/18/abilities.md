**Lakhamu Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Buffalo](../../../bestiary/buffalo/), [Crab](../../../bestiary/crab/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/) — stun + Parting Blow for the golems, Water damage on the boss.

## Abilities

- **Landslip** — golems spawn and knockback lines push you ~half the room (no pet cover); golems fire lines then become adds
- **Sand Tempest** — uncleansable blind plus exploding orbs
- **Earth Shaker** — cones on you and your pet
- **Earthrender** — three baited puddles

## How to handle it

Position for Landslip so no golem faces you after the push. War Cry stuns the golems, Parting Blow finishes them; then Crab for Water damage. Don't stack with the pet for Earth Shaker; walk out of the Earthrender puddles; stand where there are no orbs for Sand Tempest.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
