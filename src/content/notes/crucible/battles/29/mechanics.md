**Treant Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Spriggan](../../../bestiary/spriggan/) — AoE and an interrupt.

## Abilities

- **Rustling Breeze** — glowing hands = side cones (middle safe); glowing face = frontal cone (sides safe)
- **Falling Leaves** — add waves
- **Arboreal Storm** — circle expanding into donuts — knocks up; wait for the first, then stand just outside the sand
- **Acorn Bomb** — spread AoE that sleeps

### Waves

- **Slugs** — baited AoEs
- **Sapling** — gains regen + damage stacks forever — burn it fast (or Diremite to push it away)
- **Biloko** — Natural Nurture heals the boss ~5% — interrupt it
- **Diremite** — frontal lines

## How to handle it

Borrow Soul Crush, swap to Behemoth. Meteor wave one; race the Sapling down; Snarl the pet for Acorn Bomb, interrupt the Biloko's heal and focus it.
