**Progenitrix Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/), [Spriggan](../../../bestiary/spriggan/) — nothing specific — just damage.

## Abilities

- **Scalding Scolding** — jumps to a side and cleaves in front
- **Meltdown** — marker: line tankbuster that binds, knocks back and leaves three fire puddles
- **Fiery Fury** — three knockbacks from purple circles — cannot be immuned
- **Massive Explosion** — interruptible raidwide

### Adds

- **Bomb** — fire puddles on death
- **Grenade** — boss is immune while it lives; spams raidwides and gains a damage stack every time it is hit — **kill it before 16 stacks**
- **Snoll** — circle explosion on death
- **Pyrobolus** — cones in all directions

## How to handle it

Stand at her side for Scalding Scolding, kill adds as they appear (mind their death effects), and treat the Grenade as the priority.
