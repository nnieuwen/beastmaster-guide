**Drake Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/), [Karlabos](../../../bestiary/karlabos/) — AoE for the first adds, a dispel and Water damage.

## Abilities

- **Burning Cyclone** — jumps to a side, frontal cone; fireballs pulse circles and one becomes a tornado donut
- **Blaze Spikes** — dispellable damage reflect — Quelling Wave it
- **Mangling Fang** — tankbuster

### Adds

- **Barbmole** — Needles Out — reflects with a stacking bleed (not dispellable); Seeding Needles big circle
- **Abbadon** — eats Morphos for regen stacks (none if the Morpho is low HP)
- **Morpho** — stacks that Confuse at 16; confuses you if killed normally — must be eaten by the Abbadon

## How to handle it

Borrow Quelling Wave from Karlabos, swap to Behemoth. Meteor + Parting Blow the Barbmoles. Kill the Morphos before the Abbadon reaches them, then the Abbadon — or let it eat everything and kite it until the regen falls off.
