**Ice Dragon Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — DPS, plus Scouring Ash borrowed up front for Frostbite.

## Abilities

- **Icy Torment** — icicles floating = sides cleaved, middle safe (stand on the boss); ice sucked in = middle cleaved, sides safe
- **Rime Wreath** — circle with a cleansable DoT
- **Withering Eternity** — DoT floor with two safe cardinal spots, then Sheet of Ice — six baited AoEs that drop slower than you expect
- **Cauterize** — stores four half-arena cleaves, ends in a knockback; ice sprites spawn during it

## How to handle it

Withering Eternity is the hard one: get to a safe cubby, then place the Sheet of Ice drops calmly. For Cauterize read the line telegraphs, finish where the knockback can't wall you, and kill the sprites.
