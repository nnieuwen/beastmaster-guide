**Moogle Pieces** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/) — AoE and tankiness.

### Waves (timed — they start whether or not the last wave is dead)

- **1** — six Swordsmogs — AoE them
- **2** — three Mogbreakers — big circles on spawn; stand at the wall with no moogle
- **3** — Kinged Kupocasters + Kupocurers (2+1 or 1+2): Pom Flare / Pom Flood apply elemental vulnerabilities — focus one pair
- **4** — three Kupocasters — growing exaflares
- **5** — three Kupocurers — AoE in the triangle between them
- **6** — Kinged Swordsmog + Mogmugger (steals tokens, then leaves) + two Melomogs (heal/buff bubbles — step on them to pop)

## How to handle it

No trick to it: kill moogles as fast as possible.
