**Flauros Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Golem](../../../bestiary/golem/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Ghost](../../../bestiary/ghost/) — tankiness, Earth damage and a cleanse.

## Abilities

- **Heat Lightning** — three sprites casting lines — low HP, can be killed or dodged
- **Charged Lightning** — frontal half cleave — go behind
- **Electric Shock** — large circle
- **Aetherial Offering** — enlarges any surviving sprites and their lines
- **Erratic Blaster** — tankbuster with cleansable paralysis

## How to handle it

Borrow Scouring Ash from Ghost, then let Golem tank the whole fight including busters. If Erratic Blaster paralyses the Golem, swap to Worm and let it tank instead.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
