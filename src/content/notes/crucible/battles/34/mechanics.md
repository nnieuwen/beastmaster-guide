**Boogeyman Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — AoE, tankiness and a cleanse.

## Abilities

- **Provision** — swaps weapon; autos inflict a vulnerability to the *other* weapon — swap who tanks each time
- **Ripples of Gloom** — cleansable blind
- **Veil of Darkness** — boss and its bombs / deepeye go invisible unless you are close; light sprites are always visible
- **Swoop (sword)** — dash to a wall, large cone toward centre — follow and stand at its side
- **Swing Round (sword)** — large circle — move away
- **Swoop (club)** — dash to a wall, massive circle with knockback — move away
- **Swing Round (club)** — big knockback — stay close so you aren't walled; Shield Charge back

### Adds

- **Bomb** — repeated Explosion; self-destructs if ignored
- **Deepeye** — petrifying gaze
- **Sprite** — cone toward its facing when it dies — aim it at the boss to reveal it

## How to handle it

During Veil, Meteor the invisible pack (bombs first, then the deepeye) and kill a sprite facing the boss to light it up.
