**Tablitaur Pieces** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Spriggan](../../../bestiary/spriggan/), [Chimera](../../../bestiary/chimera/), [Golem](../../../bestiary/golem/) — damage (Fire from Spriggan), bulk, and two Soul Crush sources for Rallying Cheer.

## Abilities

- **1000-tonze Swipe** — half-room cleave in the facing direction
- **1111-tonze Swing** — huge circle around the caster
- **10-tonze Stomp** — knockback from a blue circle
- **100-tonze Slash** — strong line
- **Rallying Cheer** — interruptible; dispellable damage-up
- **Endless Swing** — starts in a corner and slowly spins toward you
- **Endless Swipes** — jumps to the centre and spams rotating cones

## How to handle it

Two targets, both hitting hard, casting **simultaneously** and not always in the same order — learn each mechanic rather than a sequence and watch both cast bars.

Swipe: stand behind whoever finishes first, then swap. Swing: stand near the one casting *second*, far from the first, then swap. Stomp: don't get knocked into a wall, and don't let both you and the pet eat it. Interrupt Rallying Cheer. Endless Swing + Swipes at once is the hard part — keep running from the spinner while dodging the cones.
