**Lauda Piece** — boss.

**Suggested trio:** [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — tankiness and damage; paralysis resistance helps a lot.

## Abilities

- **Vanishing Daggers** — four dagger sets fire in spawn order and turn invisible — memorise the order
- **Point Maker** — two dagger sets beside her; she and they fire baited lines — keep them off the statues
- **Unseen Force** — binds you, teleports in front or behind, throws a dagger: when the debuff ends you fly pocket-to-pocket — dagger in your **front** knocks you back, in your **back** knocks you forward
- **Deadly Demesne** — as Guttler, plus a variant where a ring of inward daggers closes on the cage
- **Molten Metal** — as Guttler
- **Thunderbolt** — as Guttler, but also gives her a stacking damage buff
- **Greater Strength** — two sword adds make her immune and stack magic damage; she spams raidwides while casting Guttler Glutter (knockback from centre) or Searing Axe (proximity from centre)

## How to handle it

Guttler again with three differences: the Thunderbolt stacks, the extra Demesne, and Unseen Force. Combine Unseen Force with her Gluttonous attacks — for Gutting stay with her and gap-close behind after the knock; for Goring just ride it to the far cubby. Kill the Greater Strength swords fast and read whether to stay close (knockback) or go far (proximity).

The ring-dagger Demesne: find the two squares drawn in corners, stand on the opposite diagonal, drop Molten Metal there and leave once the AoEs resolve.
