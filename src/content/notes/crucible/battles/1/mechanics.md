**Bone knight & bone bishop** (the "Bone Brothers").

- **Ossify** — long cast that buffs the enemy's damage. It's interruptible: send a beast with an interrupt (Soulkin Beast Mode also interrupts).
- **Death Spiral** — donut AoE. Go **inside**.
- **Tumulus** — go **outside**.
- **Black Eruption** — pulsing outward AoEs, exaflare style. Wait for two pulses, then move into where it started.
- **Forward Guard** — the knight guards its front. Send a beast in to draw aggro and hit it from behind.

Source: [First Board of the Unbroken Crucible EASY Guide (Legendary Rank & Third Degree)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0_G1NCwhJ0), via transcript.
