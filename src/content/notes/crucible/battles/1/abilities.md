**Bone Knight & Bone Bishop** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/), [Opo-opo](../../../bestiary/opo-opo/), [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/) — an interrupt, tankiness and Blunt damage.

## Abilities

- **Ossify** — interruptible damage-up buff on the Knight
- **Tumulus** — small circle around the Knight
- **Forward Guard** — Regen + Directional Parry — blocks all damage from the front
- **Death Spiral** — donut centred on the Bishop
- **Black Eruption** — circles that start on the Bishop and march outward on cardinals or intercardinals
- **Ancient Aero** — line from the Bishop; applies a cleansable Windburn DoT

## How to handle it

Two targets the whole fight, but it's the tutorial for a reason. Death Spiral opens — stand under the Bishop. Interrupt Ossify with Soul Crush when you have it. After Tumulus step away from the Knight; for Black Eruption wait for at least the second ring before moving under the Bishop.

When the Knight raises Forward Guard, juggle aggro with Snarl / Challenge so whichever of you isn't being faced is hitting its back. Enough damage drops the guard.
