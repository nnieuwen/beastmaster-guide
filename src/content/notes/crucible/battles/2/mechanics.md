**Arch demon piece.**

- **Abyssal Charge** — spawns lances that fire line AoEs.
- **Dismember** — blue floor lines; dodge into the safe zone once the first set goes off.
- **Abyssal Transfixion** — spawns daggers / crosses that explode into circular AoEs.
- **Axe attack** — frontal half-room cleave. Stay in the back half of the hitbox.

Source: [First Board of the Unbroken Crucible EASY Guide (Legendary Rank & Third Degree)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0_G1NCwhJ0), via transcript.
