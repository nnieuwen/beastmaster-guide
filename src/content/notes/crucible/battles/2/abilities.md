**Arch Demon Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Coeurl](../../../bestiary/coeurl/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/) — Lightning damage and bulk.

## Abilities

- **Abyssal Charge** — spears around the arena fire lines at you
- **Dismember** — five lines sweeping east→west or west→east, one at a time
- **Abyssal Transfixion** — swords drop circles beneath them; an axe cleaves in front of the boss

## How to handle it

Move out of the Abyssal Charge lines. For Dismember stand in the middle and step into a square that has already gone off — the timing is trickier than it looks. Move away from the hanging swords, then get behind the boss for the axe cleave.

It loops and starts layering mechanics, so burn it before that gets messy.
