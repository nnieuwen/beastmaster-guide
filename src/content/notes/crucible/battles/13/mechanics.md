**Loosefrox Inkyjots & Chewchum Popoto** — boss.

**Suggested trio:** [Salamander](../../../bestiary/salamander/), [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/), [Opo-opo](../../../bestiary/opo-opo/) — Quelling Wave for Water damage + dispel, Blunt damage, and tankiness.

## Abilities

- **Gobspin Headlops** — short unlit sword = circle (get away); long glowing sword = donut (stay close)
- **Kinborrow** — dispellable Vulnerability Down on the goblin
- **Gobbieboom Barrage** — waves of Gobbie Bombs: small round ones can be pushed by attacks (small circle); big spiky ones can't (large cross)
- **Bomb Toss** — small circle
- **Goblin Hammer** — medium circles everywhere — tiny safe spots

### Chewchum Popoto (worm)

- **Sand Breath** — frontal cone from a sandpool
- **Earthquake** — enrage

## How to handle it

Kill both around the same time or you get an endless enrage — but the worm isn't always targetable. It burrows, surfaces at a "!" pool and Sand Breaths; stand behind the pool.

After the first Barrage the worm dashes three times between pools. **Hit a round bomb so it rolls into the pool it stopped in** — it detonates, flips the worm and opens a burst window. Dispel Kinborrow with Quelling Wave each time. Later barrages mix spiky bombs in: the big ones pop first with a safe spot in the centre, then push a small one into the worm's pool.
