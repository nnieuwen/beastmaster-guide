**Voidmancer Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/), [Golem](../../../bestiary/golem/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/) — Earth damage and AoE for the Zombie Pieces.

## Abilities

- **Death Drive** — big AoE under you that raises any Zombie Pieces inside it
- **Dark Orb** — massive circle
- **Evil Mist** — orbs that pulse circles
- **Mindjack** — Forced March in the shown direction when it expires

### Zombie Piece

- **Auto-attacks** — stack Wit's End — Confusion at 12

## How to handle it

Drop Death Drive where it catches the fewest zombies (the NE and SW walls have spots that reach only one), then AoE whatever rises — Golem and Worm clear them fast. Don't let zombies chew on you.

Mindjack into Evil Mist: be positioned before the debuff ends so the march doesn't carry you into an orb.
