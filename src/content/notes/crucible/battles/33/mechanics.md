**Mindflayer Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/) — AoE and burst.

## Abilities

- **Void Water III** — spreads that leave puddles; standing in one lowers your Lightning resistance
- **Void Thunder III** — AoE tankbuster that then explodes in a cross — don't be in a puddle
- **Arcane Utterance** — four orbs fire lines along their arrows
- **Arcane Enhancement** — tethered orbs grow and fire much bigger lines
- **Void Paralysis III** — raidwide + cleansable paralysis — let the pet eat it, then swap it
- **Void Flare Stare** — proximity — corner opposite the star

### Myconid Piece

- **Spores on death** — poison zone (cleansable) — kill them inside the water puddles

## How to handle it

Worm's Bottomless Desert Heavies the boss so it takes longer to return to centre for Arcane Utterance, buying time. Note which orbs get tethered and dodge those first.
