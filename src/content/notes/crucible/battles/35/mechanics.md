**Atomos Piece** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/) — AoE and tankiness.

## Abilities

- **Mana Manifestation** — summons adds at an AoE
- **Shockwave** — knockback from the middle (pet cover works)
- **Dark II** — frontal cone
- **Forsaken Fount** — orbs pulsing AoEs; bigger orbs, bigger pulses

### Adds

- **Gremlins** — the red one applies physical vulnerability
- **Vodoriga** — baited AoEs
- **Bavarois** — knockback on death; leaves nine puddles (+Fire res, −Lightning res)
- **Pudding** — stack marker — stand in a puddle to reduce it
- **Flan** — spread marker + lots of AoEs — stand out of puddles
- **Dahak** — Tail Drive behind, Rotten Breath in front

## How to handle it

Meteor + Parting Blow the gremlins. Kill the Bavarois before the Pudding spawns and don't get knocked out.
