**Sphinx Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/) — front-loaded damage and Piercing.

## Abilities

- **Banish** — sphinx pose (front feet together) = donut; standing (feet apart) = circle; flying = half-room cleave on the glowing-wing side
- **Numeric Riddle** — raidwide, then tiles numbered 1–9
- **Assignment** — one or two debuffs — stand on a matching tile when they expire. All Prime = 2 3 5 7 · All Even = 2 4 6 8 · All Odd = 1 3 5 7 9 · All Three = 3 6 9
- **Mnemonic Riddle** — "which of these four beasts is a <kin>?" — they become mandragoras and shuffle; click the right one for a heal + 30 s damage buff, or eat paralysis and four adds
- **Lost Hope** — spinning pointer that only lets you move in its direction

## How to handle it

The poses are hard to tell apart at first — watch the feet and the wings. Track the correct mandragora through the shuffle. It should die before Lost Hope matters.
