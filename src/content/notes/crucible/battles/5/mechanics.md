**The Ogre** — the board's *elite* tile — with wisps.

- **Scorching Smite** — massive frontal cleave.
- **All Fire** — fire raid-wide plus ground AoEs and a puddle under the boss that leaves a burn.
- The ogre **channels its next cleave at one puddle, then teleports to the opposite puddle** to execute it — run to the sides of the *second* puddle.
- **Add phase** — eight wisps in total: two waves of three small and one large. Apply Slow to the greater wisps and focus the small ones first. With enough burst you can skip the phase outright.

Source: [First Board of the Unbroken Crucible EASY Guide (Legendary Rank & Third Degree)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0_G1NCwhJ0), via transcript.
