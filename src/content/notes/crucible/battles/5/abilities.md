**Ogre Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Zu](../../../bestiary/zu/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/) — damage from Zu and Colibri, tankiness and Quelling Wave from Gigantoad.

## Abilities

- **Scorching Smite** — big frontal cone from a wall
- **Allfire** — two persistent fire circles plus spammed ground circles
- **Burning Ward** — invulnerable; Wisp / Great Wisp Pieces march from the edge to the centre and explode (Vulnerability Up)
- **Fire Call** — a fireball that chases you and pops on contact
- **Straight Punch** — heavy melee hit

## How to handle it

Halatali's last boss with a twist: on the second Scorching Smite he teleports between the two fire circles mid-cast — stand behind where he will *arrive*, not where he is.

During Burning Ward kill the wisps before they reach the centre; Quelling Wave is ranged and bonus damage on them. Run from Fire Call until it despawns.
