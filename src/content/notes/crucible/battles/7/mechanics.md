**Manticore Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/), [Zu](../../../bestiary/zu/), [Ghost](../../../bestiary/ghost/) — tankiness and Wind damage.

## Abilities

- **Arm and Hammer** — cleaves the half of the room on the glowing-arm side
- **Deadly Hold** — strong unavoidable melee hit — let the pet take it
- **Hammerleap** — faces a wall and leaps to it with a big circle
- **Heads and Tails / Tails and Heads** — front-then-back or back-then-front cleaves
- **Charge and Hammer** — shows a glowing arm, telegraphs charge lines, then cleaves the glowing side and immediately the other

## How to handle it

Arm and Hammer twice in a row (opposite arms), then Deadly Hold. Run away from whichever wall he faces for Hammerleap.

Charge and Hammer: memorise the glowing arm, dodge the charge lines, stand on the safe side, then swap to the *just-cleaved* side because the other half is next.
