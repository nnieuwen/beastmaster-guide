**Ymir piece & Sahagin piece** — the fight that decides the comp.

- When the Ymir's **shell breaks**, it crawls toward another shell. It's very susceptible to **Bind and Stun**: Slime's 30-second bind and Buffalo's 15-second stun both land.
- Stun it (Buffalo Tempered Release → Parting Blow), then burst with Cu Sith's Tempered Release and an instinctual combo — that's enough to kill it before it re-shells. Parting Blow on the Sahagin, then Coeurl burns the rest.
- The alternative is bringing a Soulkin for the interrupt; bursting it down with Beastkin worked better.

Source: [Mrhappy1227 — Third Board of the Unbroken LEGENDARY Rank Clear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt519BDzQqo), via transcript.
