**Ymir Piece & Sahagin Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Buffalo](../../../bestiary/buffalo/), [Diremite](../../../bestiary/diremite/), [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/) — stun, root and an interrupt.

## Abilities

- **Paralyzing Spikes** — buff on the Sahagin: hitting it paralyses you. Up from the start and whenever the Ymir gets a new shell. Not dispellable
- **Water II** — baited circles
- **Blanket Thunder** — raidwide — interruptible (War Cry works)
- **Tsunami** — wall knockback across ~90% of the arena — no pet cover
- **Dreadwash** — interruptible Hysteria

## How to handle it

Borrow Soul Crush from Ice Golem, swap to Buffalo, and work the Ymir's shell while **never hitting the Sahagin with spikes up**. Interrupt Blanket Thunder and Dreadwash (right after Tsunami). Once the Ymir is out of its shell, Parting Blow, swap to Diremite and Silkscreen-root it; then finish the Sahagin.

Alternatives: burst the Sahagin before spikes go up, or use Quelling Wave — it doesn't reflect paralysis and your pet ignores it entirely.
