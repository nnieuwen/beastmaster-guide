**Banemite Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/) — a cleanse, damage, and Ice damage + tankiness.

## Abilities

- **Bedrock Uplift** — expanding circle from the centre
- **Deadly Thrust** — melee hit with a cleansable poison
- **Venom Web** — "Shiva circle" — centre explodes first, then the perimeter circles in spawn order

### Miteling Piece

- **Silkscreen** — line that applies Heavy

## How to handle it

Stand next to the first Bedrock Uplift circle and step into it once it pops. Cleanse Deadly Thrust. For Venom Web leave the centre, stand by the first outer circle on the far side of the rotation, and step back in after it goes off. Three Mitelings spawn later and mostly just Silkscreen.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
