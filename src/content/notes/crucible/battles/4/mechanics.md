**Banemite & miteling.**

- **Bedrock Uplift** — pulsing AoE from the centre.
- **Deadly Thrust** — tankbuster that applies poison. Cleanse it with a beast skill (Bat's Ultrasonics removes a status).
- **Venom Web** — Shiva-style sequential AoEs. Find the last one to explode, then move to the centre.

Source: [First Board of the Unbroken Crucible EASY Guide (Legendary Rank & Third Degree)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0_G1NCwhJ0), via transcript.
