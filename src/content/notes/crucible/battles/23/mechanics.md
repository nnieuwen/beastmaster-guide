**Morbol Piece** — standard enemy.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Worm](../../../bestiary/worm/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — strong AoE and a safety cleanse.

## Abilities

- **Extremely Bad Breath** — rotating cones — paralysis, blind, heavy, all cleansable
- **Tremblor** — knockback from the boss
- **Vine Probe** — frontal line

### Adds

- **Seedling** — poison on auto (cleansable)
- **Carrion** — slow, hard-hitting; Vulnerability + Heavy + Paralysis in melee (paralysis cleansable)
- **Ochu** — Acid Mist — circle that leaves a toxic puddle

## How to handle it

Borrow Scouring Ash from Geshunpest, swap to Behemoth, never stand in front, and just burn. Meteor the adds, Parting Blow the next wave, then Worm.

Source: [Icy Veins — Beastmaster Crucible of the Unbroken guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/beastmaster-crucible-of-the-unbroken).
