**Pas de Seul** (weak to Piercing) with a **succubus mage** and **succubus knight** (both weak to Fire) — the boss of the board.

- **Blood Rain** — glowing aura = donut, go **in**; no glow = go **out**.
- **Void Arrow** — stand behind the boss and dodge the orb "pizza slices".
- **Call Caress** — tankbuster with a poison DoT.
- **Summon** — spawns the adds. Burst the **mage** first so it can't buff the boss.
- **Beguiling Mist** — Sleep. Without immunity, stand in the centre of the room: that leaves enough time to run from the pheromones that spawn once the sleep ends.

Third Degree: type advantage matters here — Cu Sith (slashing/physical) into the piercing-weak boss is the pick.
