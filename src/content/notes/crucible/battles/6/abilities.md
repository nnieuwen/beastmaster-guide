**Pas de Seul** — boss.

**Suggested trio:** [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — Piercing damage, a cleanse, and Meteor for the adds.

## Abilities

- **Blood Rain** — hand raised and glowing = circle (get out); otherwise donut (stay in)
- **Void Aero II** — untelegraphed line that leaves a wind orb casting radial cones
- **Cold Caress** — cleansable poison
- **Blood Sword** — melee hit that heals her
- **Beguiling Mist** — Nightmare; two tethered hearts drift together and explode where they meet

### Succubus Mage

- **Fanaticism** — dispellable damage-up
- **Void Fire** — large circle

### Succubus Knight

- **Sweet Steel** — frontal cone

## How to handle it

Stand beside or behind her for Void Aero II, then find the gap in the orb's cones. Summon brings one Mage and two Knights — Meteor one-shots them. Move away from the hearts before they meet.
