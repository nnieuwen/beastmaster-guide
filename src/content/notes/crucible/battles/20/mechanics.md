**Cactuar Pieces** — possible triple-card fight.

**Suggested trio:** [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Buffalo](../../../bestiary/buffalo/), [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/) — AoE, tankiness, damage.

## Abilities

- **Emergency Order** — the boss jumps away and starts spawning waves
- **Healing Water** — Flowertenders AoE-heal the Sabotenders
- **??? Needles** — numbered circles that go off in order
- **Cover** — Guardia takes all damage for the Campeador
- **Seedling Needles** — raidwide when grounded, donut when airborne
- **Needle Gnarl** — circle

### Waves

- **Wave 1** — two Flowertenders + Sabotenders
- **Wave 2** — four Soldado + Sabotenders
- **Wave 3** — Guardia, Campeador + Sabotenders

## How to handle it

Meteor the first wave. War Cry the second once they're grouped, then Shield Charge to finish. Adamantoise tanks and brings Lightning for the third wave — Shield Charge the small ones, then kill Guardia before Campeador.
