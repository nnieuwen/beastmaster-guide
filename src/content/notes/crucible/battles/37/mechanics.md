**Durga Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Raptor](../../../bestiary/raptor/), [Opo-opo](../../../bestiary/opo-opo/), [Drake](../../../bestiary/drake/) — damage, Blunt for the rooks, Smoldering Scales for Atomic Ray.

## Abilities

- **Electric Absorption** — tethers three pillars and charges the hands
- **Grounding Jolt** — four AoEs per hand; the uncharged hand's is smaller — safe spot is far away on that arm's side. Rotates from the second cast
- **Missile** — tracking lines that lock when the lasers turn red — hold still, then step out
- **Thermobaric Charge** — baited corner-to-corner knockback — no pet cover; Shield Charge back
- **Diffusion Ray** — frontal cone
- **Summon** — four spinner-rooks — damage check (Opo-opo for Blunt)
- **Atomic Ray** — heavy raidwide the pet cannot cover — Smoldering Scales or a Ring of Sacrifice pet

## How to handle it

Later loops layer the same mechanics faster.
