**Guttler Piece** — boss.

**Suggested trio:** [Adamantoise](../../../bestiary/adamantoise/), [Colibri](../../../bestiary/colibri/), [Cu Sith](../../../bestiary/cu-sith/) — tankiness plus high damage; nothing specific is required.

## Abilities

- **Gyrocleave** — vertical line — stand in a side cubby
- **Beastly Aura** — knockback north and south — stand beside her to be pushed into a cubby
- **Gluttonous Goring** — floats to a side, axe hanging: sword circles, then a massive circle — be in the *opposite* cubby, move early, Sprint
- **Gluttonous Gutting** — jumps to a side, axe behind: sword circles, then a frontal cleave — be in the cubby *she* occupies, behind her
- **Thunderbolt** — line tankbuster that paralyses — pop a serum / resistance early
- **Overpowering Point** — baited line
- **Deadly Demesne** — cages (bind if you stand in one; detonate their tile + four neighbours) and daggers (hit the tile they point at)
- **Molten Metal** — baited AoE that leaves a sword with a proximity explosion — drop it in a far cubby and cross over

## How to handle it

Her autos are cones, so don't stand near the pet. The side statues wake up if line AoEs hit them — aim Thunderbolt / Overpowering Point away from them. Her order varies, so know every mechanic rather than a timeline.
