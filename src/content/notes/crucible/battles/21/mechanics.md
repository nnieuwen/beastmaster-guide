**Guttler the Gutter** (with a Thanatos piece) — the boss. Where most Legendary attempts die.

- **Autos cleave your pet.** If the pet is anywhere near the centre it gets hit. Park it on the polar opposite side with the *Place* pet command, and fight from the other side; it'll run in on its own once you attack.
- After the swords go out and the point-blanks resolve: **giant axe glowing and she's on the ground → get behind her**; **airborne, about to crash → point-blank AoE**, get out. In general, be on the opposite side from your pet so the next autos don't cleave it.
- **Tankbuster paralyzes** unless you have paralysis resistance (Crimson Ribbon). You can get paralyzed mid-dodge; there's still time, and Mystic Boots help.
- **Beastly Flare** is a proximity AoE with steep falloff — easy to forget.
- Slow-burn it. Don't Parting Blow and re-summon at a bad moment; that's how pets eat an auto and lose Selfless.
