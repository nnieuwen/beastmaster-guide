**Golem Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Gigantoad](../../../bestiary/gigantoad/), [Mantis](../../../bestiary/mantis/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/) — Water damage and tankiness from Gigantoad, DPS from the rest.

## Abilities

- **Earthen Ring** — donut — from the *other* golem if tethered
- **Plaincracker** — large circle — from the other golem if tethered
- **Rock Wall** — spiked walls: spikes facing in = get out, facing out = get in; the spike side hits and stuns
- **Stoneshower** — six rocks: normal = circle, glowing/cracked = shatter into a donut (stand under the cracked ones)
- **Outcrop** — frontal cone
- **Obliterate** — knockback (pet cover works)
- **Self-destruct** — enrage from the golem heart once both are dead

## How to handle it

Two golems that swap positions by tether — the tethered one is the source of the next attack. Order varies; watch cast bars. After both die a **heart** becomes targetable: kill it before Self-destruct.
