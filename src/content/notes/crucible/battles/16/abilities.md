**Zu Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Ice Golem](../../../bestiary/ice-golem/), [Damselfly](../../../bestiary/damselfly/), [Wespe](../../../bestiary/wespe/) — highest damage plus an interrupt.

## Abilities

- **Hatch** — two eggs hatch unless killed before the cast ends
- **Caustic Vomit** — interruptible damage buff, cast by the hatchling
- **Crossbreeze** — plus-shaped AoE
- **Flying Frenzy** — AoE tankbuster — don't drop it on the pet
- **Ferocious Fore / Rear Carve** — tethered front or back cleave; stretch it purple. Doesn't break eggs
- **Airy Pursuit** — AoEs that follow you — drop at a wall and cross to the other

## How to handle it

**The eggs are the fight.** Killing one gives Zu a permanent damage buff, and they die to almost anything including an AoE dropped on them. The hatchlings are fine to kill. So: Crossbreeze in the middle and step out, keep every AoE off the eggs, kill the two hatchlings, interrupt Caustic Vomit.
