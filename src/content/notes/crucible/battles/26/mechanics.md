**Gargoyle Piece** — elite.

**Suggested trio:** [Coblyn](../../../bestiary/coblyn/), [Behemoth](../../../bestiary/behemoth/), [Geshunpest](../../../bestiary/geshunpest/) — tankiness and damage.

## Abilities

- **Rippling Evisceration** — circle then donut
- **Sweeping Evisceration** — tether: dashes to you, cleaves front then back — stretch it purple, then go behind, then in front
- **Malady** — orbs that buff the boss; soaking gives stacks — Hysteria at five
- **Fivefold Fallout** — four raidwides ending in a knockback (pet cover works)
- **Desolation** — two casts of frontal lines
- **Grim Fate** — multi-hit tankbuster
- **Sea of Pitch** — three baited lingering puddles that Hysteria

## How to handle it

Soak the Malady orbs **4 – 4 – 2**: take four, wait for the stacks to fall off, repeat. Sweeping Evisceration follows Malady, then she centres for Fivefold Fallout. Kite Sea of Pitch around her.
