Bring the full squad (see *Team comps* above — Second Board of the Unbroken allows the cap shown in the header). This is the leveling run: everyone gains rank XP, so follow the *cap − 3* rule from the Crucible overview if you're leveling the whole team.

## Safe route

**Right** at the first fork, **right** at the second. An early campsite and the fewest possible fights. Left at the second fork adds a campsite but may cost you an extra enemy.

## Coverage roster

Zu, Ghost, Coeurl, Adamantoise, Golem, Worm, Spriggan, Chimera, Gigantoad, Opo-opo, Salamander, Cu Sith. That covers every encounter on the board — bring the lot and pick the three you need at each stop (the suggested trio is on every battle page).
