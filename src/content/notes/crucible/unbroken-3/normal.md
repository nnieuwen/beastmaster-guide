Bring the full squad (see *Team comps* above — Third Board of the Unbroken allows the cap shown in the header). This is the leveling run: everyone gains rank XP, so follow the *cap − 3* rule from the Crucible overview if you're leveling the whole team.

## Safe route

**Right**, **right**, **right** at all three forks. Two campsites and a shop. Pick up **paralysis resistance** along the way for the boss — Crimson Ribbon or a G2 Antiparalysis Soul Serum.

## Coverage roster

Squirrel, Raptor, Cu Sith, Behemoth, Buffalo, Ice Golem, Diremite, Colibri, Damselfly, Crab, Wespe, Adamantoise, Coblyn, Mantis. First level-50 board; expect a few attempts and some beast leveling.
