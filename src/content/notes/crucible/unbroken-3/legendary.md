**Team:** triple Beastkin — **Cu Sith, Buffalo, Coeurl**, all at rank 15 (the board's sync; his beast rank was 19). Coeurl is self-sufficient in its combo: lightning damage plus a lightning-resistance down that boosts its own follow-up. Cu Sith is there to burst. Buffalo was picked for exactly one job — its 15-second stun in the second fight — and the comp was built around that.

Gear in the run: weapon and hoplon +3, head +1, body +1, hands +2, legs +1, feet +2, **no food**.

## Bonuses to bank

Legendary needs ~17,500; the run scored **18.5k** with:

- **Beast Squad III** + **Beastkin Gambit** — three beasts, one classification.
- **Habitual Hoarder** (10 Crucible items, 1,000) and **Walking Armory** (10 beast gear, 500). Neither is strictly required, but together they cover one mistake on the boss. Crucible items are the more important 10.
- **Unbeastable** — never take a Vulnerability Up or Damage Down. Getting grabbed by the tethered zombie on the Siren fight, or clipped by a Dead Man's Dirge, forfeits this.
- **No Beast Left Behind** and **Selfless** — keep pets alive and at full HP. Two fights on this board (the Siren and the boss) cleave with their **auto-attacks**, and the cleave hits your pet: keep the boss facing away from it, or use the *Place* pet command to park it on the far side.
- **Economic Ethos** — no Crucible items used on the boss (the run had 10 hoarded and used none).

## Habits that carry the run

- Before almost every fight: summon a Beastkin, **Borrow** from it, then swap to the beast you'll actually open with. Beastskin is worth it here — 25% less damage taken and 5% crit for the duration.
- **Parting Blow before a tankbuster or AoE** so the pet isn't out for it — the Zu's tankbuster is an AoE and will hit a pet, unlike most.
- Don't release and re-summon at bad moments on the boss; that's how pets eat autos.

## Items and shops

- Early tiles drop a fang, a sand and an **eye**. Take the eye that boosts **territory tokens** over the one that boosts loot — buying from shops is worth more.
- Buy the **cheapest** Crucible items and beast gear (50–100 token sale items) purely to reach 10 of each. **Duplicates can't be bought**, so at the treasure near the end pick the *most expensive* item, leaving the cheap ones purchasable in the final shop.
- **Ring of Curing** early makes Legendary "a joke"; Ring of Curing + **Mirage Vest** and there's no chance you miss. With that much sustain you can go full offensive on beast gear.
- **Vampiric Fang** (heal), **Beast Potion** (heal, can proc auto-potion), **Mystic Boots** (movement — helps with the paralyze on the boss), **Crimson Ribbon** (paralysis resistance for the boss tankbuster). A blind or petrification potion usually drops off the second boss.
- Shop-free runs are fine on boards 1–2 but not from the third board on — you want both 10s.

## Route and fights

Enemy 1 → enemy 2 (Ymir) → shop → **Zu** (the elite fork, not the Catoblepas) → Lakhamu → treasure → **Siren** (elite) → treasure → shop → boss. Mechanics for each are on the battle pages (see *Battles on this board* below).

Board 2 is the easiest of the three to optimise; this one is harder mainly because of the boss's pet-cleaving autos.
