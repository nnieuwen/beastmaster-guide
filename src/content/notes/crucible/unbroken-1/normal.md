Bring the full squad (see *Team comps* above — First Board of the Unbroken allows the cap shown in the header). This is the leveling run: everyone gains rank XP, so follow the *cap − 3* rule from the Crucible overview if you're leveling the whole team.

## Safe route

**Left** at the first fork, **right** at the second. Two campsites, so you can heal twice. Going left at either fork instead trades a camp for an extra item.

## Coverage roster

Ice Golem, Opo-opo, Gigantoad, Coeurl, Geshunpest, Behemoth, Cu Sith, Zu, Adamantoise, Colibri. Ice Golem, Gigantoad, Behemoth, Cu Sith and Adamantoise can all tank for you if HP management is the problem.
