**Team:** Vilekin trio — Diremite, Mantis, Wespe (all rank 5). Two alternatives from the *Legendary Rank & Third Degree* guide also work: a Cloudkin trio (Bat, Vulture, Zu — two INT attackers) and a Vilekin variant with Damselfly in for Diremite. All three are under *Team comps* above; load one into the builder from there.

Sold as a Faded Remnant farm: Legendary every time, 10 per run.

## Setups that work

- **Cloudkin (utility / cleanse):** Zu for wind resistance down and main damage, Vulture for wind damage and cleansing enemy buffs, Bat to cleanse your own poisons and bleeds.
- **Burst (damage):** Behemoth's Meteor for add phases, Cu Sith and Opo-opo as auto-attackers with burst.
- **Vilekin (poison):** stack poisons and buff Wespe's Final Sting.

## Tricks from the Legendary run

- **Free Borrow:** summon a sacrificial beast first, use Borrow, then swap to your main beast — you keep both Tempered Release and Borrow available on the first cycle.
- Use **Threaten** so your beasts take as little auto-attack damage as possible (No Beast Left Behind and Selfless are worth 2,000 together).
- Skip add phases with burst. The demonstrated run scored about **17.9k** for Legendary.

## Third Degree

Priorities flip to pure survival. Treat your second and third beasts as sacrificial tanks for tankbusters, lean on elemental and type advantages (Cu Sith into the piercing-weak boss), and watch for drops like the Angel Robe for poison immunity.
