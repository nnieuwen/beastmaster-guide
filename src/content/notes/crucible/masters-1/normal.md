Bring the full squad (see *Team comps* above — First Master's Board allows the cap shown in the header). This is the leveling run: everyone gains rank XP, so follow the *cap − 3* rule from the Crucible overview if you're leveling the whole team.

## Safe route

**Right** at the first fork, **left** at the second, **right** at the third. Possibly skips a fight, guarantees a campsite and a second shop, and only one normal enemy before the boss. Take any **poison resistance** you see — an Angel Robe makes Borgny trivial.

## Coverage roster

Mantis, Damselfly, Wespe, Behemoth, Worm, Geshunpest, Coblyn, Adamantoise, Diremite, Drake, Gigantoad, Spriggan. Arguably easier than the third board, but gear upgrades start to matter here (IL 120 sync).
