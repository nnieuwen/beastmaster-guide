Set on xivgear: [Beastmaster BiS](https://xivgear.app/sl/f3065532-a551-4083-9444-5a02ceb0aab8).

**Left side** is the level 90 Beastmaster artifact set (Horns / Furs / Armguards / Slops / Boots, all **+4**, i130) — it's equal to i130 gear, so there's nothing to chase there. **Right side** is a mix of Augmented Ironworks and Dreadwyrm accessories.

| Slot | BiS | No-achievement BiS |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Beastliege's Hand Axe (i135) | Guttler (i135 relic) |
| Off-hand | Beastliege's Hoplon (i135) | Kornago Hoplon (i135 relic) |
| Head / Body / Hands / Legs / Feet | Beastmaster's artifact +4 (i130) | same |
| Ears | Beastmaster's Earrings (i135) | Dreadwyrm Earring of Fending |
| Neck | Dreadwyrm Choker of Fending | same |
| Wrist | Aug. Ironworks Bracelet of Slaying | same |
| Rings | Aug. Ironworks Ring of Slaying + Dreadwyrm Ring of Slaying | same |
| Food | Rock-fisted Popoto Stew | same |

Melds on the BiS set total **9× Critical Hit IV, 4× Determination IV, 2× Direct Hit IV**. The Beastliege axe + shield are the same stats as Guttler Unleashed + Augmented Kornago.

**Upgrade order:** weapon → armor → shield. The full BiS conversion takes **416 Bright Remnants of Resilience** (832 Faded Remnants).

**Avoid** Azeyma's Earrings and Neophyte's Ring for synced level 50 Crucible: they sync to lower STR and VIT inside the instance.

**Food** — anything that matches Popoto Stew's stats works:

- Sykon Bavarois
- Archon Burger
- Melon Pie
- Honeyed Dragonfruit
- Driftwood Catfish Pie
- Vegetable Soup
- Roast Chicken
- Pães de Queijo
