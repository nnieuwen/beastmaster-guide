## Before going in

- The Crucible is Beastmaster's solo content, unlocked by the level 30 job quest **Into the Crucible**. It's also **how you level captured beasts** — the max beast rank is 25, which is the rank sync of the last board.
- There are **five boards**: three *Unbroken* boards, unlocked at job level 30 / 40 / 50 (each gated behind the job quest that needs the previous board cleared), and two *Master's* boards that open after you've beaten the Unbroken three.
- Each board has its own squad cap — from 10 on the first board up to 15 on the last — and you can bring anywhere from zero up to that.
- You can **preview every tile** before committing: enemy weaknesses, the mechanics you'll face, where the shops and campsites are. Build your team against the board, not in the abstract. Clicking an enemy shows what it's weak to (element **and** physical/magical), whether its casts can be interrupted, and which statuses land on it — the board is a knowledge check far more than a damage check.
- **No outside potions** inside the Crucible, and Beastmaster has no role actions anywhere (no Second Wind, Bloodbath or Arm's Length). There's almost no sustain, so the mode is built around not taking damage: crowd control and aggro management are your health bar.
- Two duty actions: **Challenge** forces the enemy onto you; **Snarl** makes your familiar the tank and, though the tooltip doesn't say so, **nullifies your own enmity**. Your beast's HP is a resource — juggle aggro so it eats the hits you can't avoid.
- Beasts **deselect after every encounter.** Re-pick them each time; walking into a boss with empty horns because you clicked ready too fast is a well-trodden way to lose a run.
- Anything you're still holding at the end vanishes. Spend it.

## On the board

Encounters are static. Once you step onto a tile you can't backtrack until the run is complete.

| Tile | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Enemy** | Fight an enemy beast. The preview shows its weaknesses (Blunt / Slashing / Fire / Ice…) and 1–2 mechanics. |
| **Treasure coffer** | A random selection of four items; pick one. |
| **Campsite** | A healing pool split between you and however many beasts you pick: yourself alone for **90%**, you + one beast for 45% each, you + two for 30% each. If the beasts are fine, heal only yourself. Also feeds beasts. |
| **Shop** | Sells feed, consumables and beast gear. **Scroll down** — the six feed items at the top are not the whole stock, and almost everything worth buying is below the fold. |
| **Triple card** | Three face-down "?" cards; can resolve into any of the above. |
| **Boss** | The final tile of the board. |

## Score, rank and Legendary

Clearing a board grants a rank based on your score — Adept, Exemplary, Apex, Legendary and so on. **Legendary** is the one that matters: it's what feeds the BiS weapon grind. The breakpoints depend on the board and on Crucible Mode; the mode bonuses unlock once you've cleared the second Master's board, with First Degree worth +4,000 and Third Degree +9,000.

| Board | Legendary breakpoint (observed) |
|---|---|
| First Board of the Unbroken | 17,439 – 17,680 |
| Second Board of the Unbroken | 17,082 – 17,605 |
| Third Board of the Unbroken | 17,851 – 17,875 |
| First Master's Board | 18,625 – 18,766 |
| Second Master's Board | 18,250 – 18,500 (lower than the first Master's board) |

### What the bonuses cover

- **Roster size** — separate bonuses for 1–3, 4–5 or 6–7 familiars. It counts what you *bring*, not what you use, so spare beasts along for XP cost you this.
- **Survival** — no incapacitated familiars, everyone at full HP, you at full HP, no Damage Down / Vulnerability Up. Your own HP at the end scores partial points even if it isn't full, so heal up before the last hit lands.
- **Restrictions** — no campsite, no feeding, no coins spent, no items used, no beast gear. Several have *inverted* versions that reward heavy use, which is why hoarding 10 items pays.
- **Kin Gambit** — a single-kin team. Feeds the Gambit achievements and titles on the Second Master's Board.
- **Universality** — full combo 5 / 10 / 15 times. Fights are usually too short for it to matter.
- **Degrees** — clearing under First, Second or Third Degree.

Every bonus and its value is under *View Bonuses* on the board screen, before or during a run. **Temporal Sand** fully resets an encounter — fumble an Unbeastable run mid-fight, wipe on purpose, and keep the bonus.

### Degrees

Once all five boards and the job quests are done, Degrees add enemy HP and damage across three levels. **Third Degree** on every board is what the *Unbroken Endgame* achievement (the Beastmaster's Earrings) wants, and it's the entry requirement for the datacenter-wide Crucible rankings.

## Leveling beasts

- Don't bring beasts that would rank-sync on the board you're doing (with one exception: a rank-1 carry beast on the Legendary trios below). **Above the sync you take an XP penalty that gets worse the further past it you are; below the sync you get nothing extra.** So: under rank 5 to board one, under 10 to board two, and so on.
- XP goes up with score, with actually clearing, and with **fewer beasts brought**. **G1 / G2 Primafodder** feed adds a flat XP bonus.
- A scoring run and a leveling run are different activities: scoring wants three beasts and no feed; leveling wants a full roster, XP feed and the fastest clear you can manage.
- **Quantity over quality.** Legendary runs give more XP per run, but if they take much longer than an Apex run, Apex is the better rate.
- Recent testing suggests a slightly **smaller team** gets more rank XP per run than a full squad.
- If you're leveling everyone on the team, bring **cap − 3**. If you're bringing a three-beast team to carry the run and leveling the rest, bring **cap − 2**.

## Rewards

- **Bright and Faded Remnants** — job-gear upgrade currency (two Faded → one Bright), with part of the weekly income capped through the Challenge Log. Also buys vanity items.
- **Gourds** — buy beasts outright. Every duty-locked beast is purchasable this way, and the stock expands after clearing the second board.
- Weapon glamours from achievements; a **beast glamour** for every beast that hits rank 25.
- Leaderboards are datacenter-wide with achievements for placing; the first season starts two weeks after release.
