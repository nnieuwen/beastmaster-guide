Beastmaster is a **limited melee DPS job** in the same mould as Blue Mage: instead of learning a fixed kit, you capture monsters and command their abilities. Being limited cuts both ways — it gets its own exclusive content, the **Crucible of the Unbroken**, but it can't queue into Ultimates, Criterion dungeons or Field Operations.

**To unlock it** you need the A Realm Reborn main scenario finished and any job at level 50 or higher. Then talk to the Excited Adventurer in New Gridania (X: 11.8, Y: 13.6).

**Job quests** come at levels 1, 8, 18, 30, 40 and 50. From 30 onward they're gated on the Crucible: the level 30 quest wants the first board cleared, the level 40 quest the second, and level 50 wants boards three through five.
